Visian® ICL™

STAAR® Surgical's phakic IOL for myopia, hyperopia and astigmatism

The Visian ICL is made from Collamer, a biocompatible material created and used exclusively by STAAR Surgical. This lens is able to treat a large range of refractive error varying by region. Contact us to see if the product is available in your region.

*Visian ICL for hyperopia is not available in the United States.
